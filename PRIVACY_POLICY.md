# Privacy Policy for Dice Roller

**Effective date:** March 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Dice Roller** mobile app (package: `com.sulemangul.dice_roller`) handles information.

## Summary
- Dice Roller is designed to work without user accounts and without collecting personal information directly.
- The app stores gameplay/settings preferences locally on your device.
- The app may connect to app store/update services to check whether a newer app version is available.

## Information We Collect
### 1) Information stored locally on your device
Dice Roller saves app preferences locally using on-device storage (SharedPreferences), such as:
- Sound on/off
- Theme mode
- Dice size and dice limit
- Number of players
- Visual/color effect settings
- Other gameplay configuration values

This data stays on your device and is used only to restore your preferences.

### 2) Information not directly collected by us
We do **not** require account registration and do **not** directly collect personal data such as:
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Precise location
- Contacts
- Photos/media files

## Third-Party Services
Dice Roller uses third-party packages/libraries for app functionality, including:
- Flutter framework and related platform components
- `upgrader` (app update check behavior)
- `audioplayers` (local audio playback)
- `package_info_plus` (app version/build info)

When update-check functionality runs, app store/platform services may process technical request data (for example, IP address or user agent) according to their own privacy policies. We do not control third-party processing by those services.

## Permissions
Dice Roller does not request sensitive runtime permissions (such as camera, microphone, location, contacts, or SMS) for its core functionality.

## How We Use Data
We use locally stored settings only to:
- Remember your app preferences
- Improve user experience across app launches
- Display app version information

## Data Sharing
We do not sell personal data and we do not share user personal information for advertising.

## Data Retention and Deletion
- Preference data remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app.
- Uninstalling the app typically removes local app data from your device.

## Children’s Privacy
Dice Roller is a casual game utility and is not intended to collect personal information from children.

## Security
We use reasonable measures appropriate for a lightweight offline-first app. No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## International Users
If third-party platform services are used for update checks, processing may occur on infrastructure in different countries according to those providers’ terms.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted with a new effective date.
